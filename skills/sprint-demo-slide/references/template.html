1 One question, on the new engine, against real data
"Which Water brands lead by mention volume in the last 60 days, and how positive is the sentiment?"
query · clickhouse dev SELECT dictGet('bank.entity_dict', 'name', entity_id) AS brand, count() AS mentions, round(avg(sentiment = 'positive') * 100, 1) AS pct_positive FROM gold.social_mentions -- the flat OBT, in production WHERE post_date >= today() - 30 GROUP BY entity_id ORDER BY mentions DESC LIMIT 10;
